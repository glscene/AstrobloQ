Contribution and collaborating

You don't have to stop here.  Using BioSphere is just step one.  You can also choose to develop with BioSphere.  Since this is an OpenSource project, anyone can add new creatures or you can add new features.  You can write about BioSphere.  Many others have tested, fixed, and visually enhanced this program. Your imagination is your limit!
